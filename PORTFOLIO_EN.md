# Al-Shafi'i Quranic School CMS 🕋✨
**A Comprehensive SaaS solution for managing Quranic circles and schools with cutting-edge web technologies.**

---

## 🌟 Project Overview
"Al-Shafi'i CMS" is more than just a student management app; it's a **complete digital ecosystem** designed to automate every aspect of Quranic school operations. It blends administrative and financial precision with an engaging educational experience, utilizing Gamification and Artificial Intelligence.

## 🛠 Technical Stack
Built with a modern stack to ensure performance, security, and scalability:
- **Frontend:**
  - `Next.js 15` & `React 19` (Latest version) for blazing-fast performance and seamless UX.
  - `Tailwind CSS` with **Glassmorphism** design for a premium, modern look.
  - `Framer Motion` for smooth interactions and visual engagement.
- **Backend:**
  - `Firebase` (Realtime Database, Authentication, Firestore) for real-time data synchronization.
- **Smart Integration:**
  - Integrated **Gemini AI 2.0 Flash** via `GenKit` for student performance analysis and smart insights.
- **Mobile Compatibility:**
  - Full `PWA` support and `Capacitor` for native-like experience on Android and iOS.
- **Additional Tools:** `Recharts` for analytics, `jspdf` for report generation, `XLSX` for data export.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1️⃣ Specialized Dashboards
- **Management Hub:** Provides a 360-degree view of all groups, students, and financial flows for administrators.
- **Sheikh/Teacher Portal:** A dedicated interface for teachers to manage circles, track attendance, and log memorization progress with a single click.

### 2️⃣ Precise Educational Tracking
- **Memorization & Revision Logs:** Granular tracking of Surahs and Ayahs, with daily evaluations (Excellent, Good, etc.).
- **Performance Radar:** Visual radar charts highlighting student strengths and weaknesses in (Hifz, Revision, Attendance, Behavior).

### 3️⃣ Gamification System
- **Excellence League:** An automated point and ranking system for students and groups to foster healthy competition.
- **Hall of Fame:** Automatic showcase of top-performing students based on advanced algorithms.

### 4️⃣ Finance & Documentation
- **Automated Invoicing:** Generates professional PDF receipts and tracks school dues.
- **Document Center:** Generates certificates, performance reports, and printable attendance sheets.

### 5️⃣ AI & Notifications
- **AI Insights:** A smart system that predicts performance drops and recommends personalized revision plans.
- **Real-time Notifications:** Push notifications for teachers and parents.
